# v0.16.17 — Readable Grip decisions without line breaks

## Changes

- Make new native Grip questions readable even when OpenCode collapses newlines and blank lines. Icons, textual labels and visible ` ┃ ` separators distinguish the retained original-request excerpt, current decision and reason. English uses matching labels; meaning does not depend on emoji rendering or color.
- Instruct Mission controllers to keep the decision and reason concise while preserving material targets, actions, exclusions and cost/retention conditions. Detailed background can precede the Form in ordinary chat, without another question. The host does not truncate authorization conditions or infer semantic sections inside arbitrary prose.
- Preserve normal option labels/descriptions/order, the permanent consultation choice, saved native question identity, free-form steering and hold/resume behavior. Already saved/open offers retain their old text and accept matching legacy retries. Only new offers receive the new display format.
