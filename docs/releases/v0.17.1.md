# v0.17.1 — Prelaunch recovery and test-debt routing

Prelaunch refusals can be reconciled with matching settled Worker and direct-operation admissions, including failed Workers. Recovery preserves the original Mission and its evidence while continuing to reject active, unknown, contradictory, or duplicate executions. Review-correction plans retain the effective validation recipe and applicable proof mappings.

Test-debt routing recognizes supported Godot test paths and selects an authorized owner when one is available. Candidate signals are not proof of cleanup, and non-equivalent validations do not share a PASS. In the examined production history, no Guard/JOIN time saving was observed; this release does not claim measured production savings.

The runtime asset marker is `0.17.1-prelaunch-recovery-v1`. Installing a package does not change an already-running shared host; restart remains a separate manual operation. Fixture results do not establish recovery of the original production Mission or an actual model-backed CLI startup.
