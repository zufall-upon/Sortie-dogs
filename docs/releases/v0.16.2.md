# v0.16.2 — Direct validation registration continuity

## Included change

- Integrates PR #320. An owning, quiescent direct controller can correct its validation recipe or cwd without replacing the existing Mission/run/unit/admission/reservation or resetting cumulative spend. The immutable contract and historical failed checks remain intact.
- New or changed registrations require actual native checks started after registration. Earlier successful diagnostics are not promoted into formal proof. Source/candidate freshness, other live or unproven writers, current-candidate Review and final acceptance remain authoritative.
- The effective recipe is shared by native capture, finish, Review, recovery and direct-to-Worker handoff. Reuse only the exact passing occurrences of an inherited prefix; do not skip an unexecuted middle check or hide a later native failure.
- V2 handoff guidance uses `executor: "worker"` with `units: ""`; the legacy API omits `units`. `units: []` remains invalid. Handoff continues the same unit and reservation; it is not validation or completion.
