# v0.16.0 — Purpose-aligned autonomy

## Scope

- Optional bounded purpose decisions distinguish vision/outcomes from methods. Decisions retain observed outcomes, concrete unmet work and the next useful action across the same Mission's handoff/resume.
- Save observations alongside already-needed planning, submission or Review operations; do not create logging-only work. Fast-lane roots can use their required Review operation without a Coordinator-only submission API.
- Decisions are advisory observations, not execution permission, validation, independent Review or final acceptance. Existing budgets, ownership, evidence freshness and quality controls remain authoritative.

## Observed behavior and limits

- Existing-output reuse reached formal validation, independent Review and acceptance on the original S1 candidate. Two historical refusals remain recorded; that run was not clean.
- The corrected S2 candidate preserved the same root/Mission/requirements/decision across a safe checkpoint and private-server restart, then performed the required real rollback and canonical validation. No generation or stage check was replayed after resume. The required Review operation recorded the decision without an extra logging-only plan or Review.
- S2 repaired a read/write declaration before checkpoint and refreshed one stage check. This is retained as an efficiency limitation, not a clean-run claim. The fixture's low-risk Review skip is distinct from the independent source Review PASS.
- Both historical outer runners exited 1 because of evaluator failures. Original results and failed receipts remain immutable; a separate provenance-bound assessment evaluates collected native outcomes without rerunning model work.
- Known converted CLI cost across these observed roots: $0.792704, with no unpriced requests. This is not total billing. The same local $3 allowance was carried forward; checkpoint/resume usage was counted once.
- These small fixtures do not establish a general success-rate, speed or cost improvement. S1 used a Worker route; S2 used a direct SOL route. Route differences are not attributed to the mechanism.
